    What counts

    First-party public HTML or a first-party legal PDF on the company’s own host. Fetch-check. No invented companies, certs, processors, or URLs.

    What stays open

    JS shells, HTTP 403/404, login walls, regulation-only GDPR/CCPA rights text, DPF as a transfer among SCCs, portal hosts. Missing prints not on file. When unsure, leave open.

    Discovery

    First-party only: candidate paths, the company’s own footer links, and its sitemaps. A lead is not a finding until it passes the first three rules.

    Outbound

    Outbound is human-gated. Documents stay on the company’s domain.
