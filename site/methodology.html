Method

How we count a public file. Not a company grade.

The clerk's rules

  1. First-party public HTML, or a first-party legal PDF, on the company’s own host.
  2. Every source is fetch-checked.
  3. Nothing invented: no companies, certs, processors, or URLs.
  4. Missing private evidence is inconclusive. It prints not on file.
  5. When unsure, leave open.
  6. Outbound is human-gated. Documents stay on the company’s domain.
  7. Discovery is first-party only: candidate paths, the company’s own footer links, and its sitemaps. A lead is not a finding until rules 1–3 pass.

Quote a rule by its anchor: opentrust.center/methodology.html#rule-4

The count, defined

printed — the signal is observed on the page. on file, not extracted — listed by our crawl, not yet parsed into the row. missing — expected and unfetchable this sweep; it prints not on file, never an X. 100 is five prints.
